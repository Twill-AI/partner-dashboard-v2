My take
A · Inter — safest choice. What Linear, Figma, and most premium SaaS products use. Invisible in the best way. Zero personality but zero friction.

B · DM Sans — already in Twill's brand guidelines. Geometric and calm. Slightly more character than Inter. Good continuity with existing brand materials.

C · Plus Jakarta Sans — my personal recommendation. Warmer and more modern than Inter, slightly rounded terminals that feel approachable without being soft. Pairs beautifully with Inter for data. Very popular in fintech right now.

D · Public Sans — institutional trust. Feels like a bank, in a good way. Neutral and reliable. Great for B2B where credibility matters more than personality.

E · Outfit — contemporary geometric. Clean and modern. More personality than Inter but less warm than Plus Jakarta. Good middle ground.
